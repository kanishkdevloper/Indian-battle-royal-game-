Dustline - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

Summary: Dustline is an offline game. It does not collect, transmit, sell or share your personal information. Everything the game saves stays on your device.

1. Who we are

Dustline (package com.dustline.game) is published by YOUR NAME OR STUDIO NAME. Contact: YOUR_EMAIL.

2. What is stored on your device

To run the game, Dustline stores the following on your device only:

  • the display name you type and a player ID that the game generates;
  • your chosen character, outfit and profile settings;
  • game progress and match statistics;
  • settings such as graphics options, sensitivity and HUD layout;
  • pictures you take with the in-game photo album, kept in the app’s private storage.

This information is never sent to us or to anyone else by the app.

3. Permissions

The app asks for one permission only: VIBRATE, to vibrate your device during play. It does not request internet access, storage, camera, microphone, contacts, location or any other sensitive permission.

4. No ads, analytics or third parties

Dustline contains no advertising, no analytics, no crash-reporting service, no social-login and no other third-party SDKs. There are no in-app purchases.

5. Sharing that you choose

The profile screen has Copy and Share buttons. Copy places a short text with your profile details on the clipboard. Share opens the standard Android share sheet with the same text; nothing is sent unless you pick an app and a recipient yourself.

6. Device backups

If backups are turned on in your Android settings, Android may include the game’s saved progress in your device or Google-account backup so it can be restored on a new phone. This is controlled by your device and your Google account, not by us.

7. Deleting your data

Uninstalling Dustline, or choosing Clear storage for the app in Android settings, deletes all data the app keeps on your device. Backups made by Android can be managed in your Google account settings.

8. Children

Dustline is not directed to children under 13. Because the app collects no personal information, we do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

9. Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects privacy (for example if online features are ever added), this page will be updated and the date above will change.
